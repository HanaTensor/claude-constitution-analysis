## Strength

F01, F02, and F03 all identify specific provisions and answer their objections.
Strength runs F01 > F03 > F02. F02's objection R1 — that the asymmetry may be a
deliberate design choice — is unresolved, so F02 may be **an unexplained asymmetry
rather than a defect**.

## The limit on all of it

In every case the claim is **not the discovery of a phenomenon** but the observation
that **this document does not regulate it**.

- The general form of F01 — that summed local alignment is not system-level
  responsibility — is already argued in arXiv:2510.14008.
- The operational hazard behind F01 is routine in practitioner guidance.
- The research area behind F02 (agents reporting on agents) is active.

Do not let the claim widen past the text.
